There's no rule that forces a player to stand at any point — you can hit as many times as you want until you either bust (go over 21) or choose to stand yourself.

  That said, basic strategy (the mathematically optimal play) gives general guidelines:

  ┌─────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Your hand  │            Recommended action            │
  ├─────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 17+         │ Always stand                             │
  ├─────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 13–16       │ Stand if dealer shows 2–6, otherwise hit │
  ├─────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 12          │ Stand if dealer shows 4–6, otherwise hit │
  ├─────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 11 or below │ Always hit (can't bust on one card)      │
  └─────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘

  The logic is based on the dealer's visible card — since the dealer must hit until 17, a low dealer card (2–6) means they're likely to bust, so you stand and let them self-destruct.

  It's purely the player's decision in the game — the HIT/STAND buttons are always available to you (as long as you're under 21).
